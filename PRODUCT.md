# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro static website, GitHub repository kmndhyn/jtlb-repair-clinic, Vercel hosting.

## Users
Gamers seeking console, controller, handheld and retro-device repair at J The Last Boss Repair Clinic.

## Product Purpose
Explain repair services, establish the JTLB identity and help visitors prepare a repair enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static site. A hero ticket selects the device. Service links carry symptoms into the enquiry. The form validates the required device and description, includes an optional model and a Repair/Upgrade choice, then copies a message tailored to the request. A manual-copy fallback handles unavailable clipboard access. The form does not submit a booking; verified direct contact details are still needed. The user authorized clearly labelled contact placeholders if an official channel could not be found. A focused web search did not establish an official booking channel; show “Contact details coming soon” rather than an invented phone or messaging URL.

## Brand Commitments
The user explicitly prefers Simon Biala's site at https://simonbiala-art.github.io/circuitfix-website/ and requires red and black. Keep its tactile repair-shop character and improve its craft rather than changing genre. Original JTLB artwork is sourced from the supplied reference repository. See DESIGN.md.

## Evidence on Hand
The reference provides brand artwork, a YouTube link and six service categories: consoles, controllers, HDMI/charging ports, cleaning/thermal, disc drives and retro/handheld. Its phone, email and street address are placeholders. The user explicitly requested these reference policies in the website copy: free diagnosis, no fix/no fee, an upfront agreed quote, a 90-day repair warranty, and most controller jobs fixed in 48 hours. Use the controller qualifier whenever presenting the 48-hour timing. Upgrade enquiries are supported; confirm compatibility, parts and pricing individually. Repair statistics and testimonials remain unverified; do not invent them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic landmarks, skip link, keyboard focus, native form validation, labelled controls, native mobile menu, announcement of copy status, reduced-motion support and a pause control for the scrolling device strip. Keep the page usable without horizontal document overflow.
